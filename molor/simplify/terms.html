The formula simplifier at valkon.us/molor/simplify is a free research tool run by FireEdge (Valentin Konovalov). By using it you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use it.

In short: the results are predictions from a model, not measurements and not safety advice. Smell and test everything yourself. Before anything you make goes on skin or is sold, you are responsible for its safety and its regulatory compliance.

1. What the tool does

You paste a formula and the page suggests a smaller blend whose predicted smell is closest to it. The predictions come from a machine-learning model trained on published odour descriptions. The doses come from odour detection thresholds, many of which are estimated and can be off by a factor of ten or more. The page explains its limits next to each result.

2. Predictions, not measurements

  • A suggested blend may smell noticeably different from your formula. The model reads a blend's bulk character and cannot see trace materials, which often matter most.
  • Proportions, dilutions and similarity scores are estimates. Use them as a starting point for your own trials, not as a finished formula.
  • The tool can be wrong, incomplete or out of date, and may change or go offline without notice.

3. Safety and regulation are your responsibility

  • Any IFRA figures, use levels or warnings the page shows are for information only. They may be incomplete, out of date, or not apply to your product type or concentration. They are not a safety assessment.
  • Aroma materials can be sensitising, irritating, toxic or flammable. Handle them following each supplier's safety data sheet.
  • Compliance with the IFRA Standards, allergen labelling and the cosmetic and chemical rules where you make or sell a product (for example EU Cosmetics Regulation 1223/2009, UK and US rules) is yours alone. Get a qualified safety assessment before selling a product or applying it to skin.

4. Your formulas and privacy

  • The simplifier runs entirely in your browser. Formulas you paste and the results are not uploaded to us or anyone else, and we don't store them. The "Show … on the atlas" links carry the blend after the # in the URL, which browsers don't send to the server.
  • Your formulas, and the blends the tool suggests for them, are yours to use for any purpose, including commercial use.
  • We count page visits with Mixpanel, an analytics service. It stores a random identifier in your browser and receives your IP address, which it uses for approximate location and does not keep. It does not see your formulas. You can block it with any common content blocker; the tool works the same without it.
